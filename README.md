## How Mirio: Magic Journey works

The core loop is short and easy to learn:

- **Move:** steer your hero with an on-screen joystick in any direction.
- **Attack:** your character hits nearby enemies automatically. Your job is positioning, not tapping.
- **Survive:** enemies keep spawning in bigger groups, move faster over time, and come from every side.
- **Adapt:** rocks, obstacles, and enemy attacks force you to keep moving.
- **Power up:** collect boosts for damage, speed, or survivability, and trigger special skills to clear a crowd or escape.

Each level lasts longer and gets more intense than the last. Defeating enemies earns points and raises your survival score.
